Attic to App: The Workflow

01. Initial Evaluation & Triage

Not every record is a candidate for restoration. We begin with a physical audit to set proper expectations. If media is severely warped or heat-damaged, we provide a transparent assessment of what can—and cannot—be saved.

02. Specialized Analog Restoration

Honesty is our policy. We use specialized wet-vacuum systems for vinyl and head-cleaning protocols for magnetic tape to achieve the cleanest possible signal without altering the soul of the original performance.

03. High-Resolution Archiving

Once cleaned, the media is played once on studio-grade equipment and captured at 24-bit/96kHz resolution. These "Gold Masters" are stored in our secure archive to ensure the signal is preserved forever.

04. The Encore Studio Experience

Through our partners at Encore Studio, we provide a direct-to-fan hub where fans can listen for free or purchase digital albums. This serves as the social heart of the project.

05. Worldwide Broadcasting

We push your legacy to over 150 digital service providers globally, including Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music, ensuring your music is only a click away anywhere in the world.

06. Reinvestment & Cultural Revival

A portion of every sale is funneled back into the WPA Legacy Fund, buying instruments for schools and funding scholarships to ensure the next generation keeps the music alive.
