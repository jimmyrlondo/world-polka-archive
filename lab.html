Restoration Standards & Technical Specifications

01. Signal Purity

We prioritize the original analog signal. By using the Universal Audio Volt 476, we capture the natural warmth of the accordion and the resonance of the brass with 24-bit precision.

02. Mechanical Restoration

Our lab utilizes specialized vacuum cleaning for vinyl and shellac, and surgical-grade head cleaning for magnetic tapes. We remove the debris of decades, not the soul of the music.

03. The Metadata Wiki

Every recording is cross-referenced against our growing Polka Wiki database. We track matrix numbers, pressing plants, and session musicians to build a complete historical map.

Building the Polka Wiki

Archiving isn't just about sound; it's about **data**. For every submission, we generate a forensic metadata profile. This information fuels our automated "Wiki" engine, ensuring that every artist and session musician is documented and discoverable.
