A Joint Initiative Proposal

Preserving the American Polka Canon

Prepared for the Leadership and Trustees of the Polka Hall of Fame

Executive Summary

The rich history of our music—spanning Cleveland Style, Chicago Style, and regional heritage recordings—is currently locked on deteriorating physical media. The World Polka Archive (WPA) proposes a digital alliance to systematically restore, digitize, and broadcast these master works to a modern global audience, securing both the cultural legacy and a new recurring revenue stream for physical preservation.

The Collaboration Framework

1. Zero-Cost Technical Restoration

WPA absorbs all technical overhead, equipment maintenance, and labor costs. Utilizing studio-grade wet-vacuum cleaning and high-resolution 24-bit capture at the North Bloomfield Lab, we digitize legacy collections safely and transparently.

2. The Digital Encyclopedia & Wiki

Every track is documented using clean, systematic spreadsheets converted to an open-access Polka Wiki. This ensures session musicians, regional labels, and accurate songwriting credits are preserved in an unalterable digital ledger.

3. Direct-to-Fan Sustenance

Restored music is made available worldwide via our partners at Encore Studio and 150+ major streaming networks. Net digital proceeds fuel the WPA Legacy Fund, directly subsidizing museum operations and heritage instrument library programs for schools.

Operational & Revenue Governance

To guarantee complete transparency for families and estates, physical intake utilizes a sliding-scale option. Estates can balance upfront restoration fees against long-term royalty splits, ensuring every demographic has access to preservation. Full composition and master copyright remain completely with the original artists and heirs.
