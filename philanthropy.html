Preserve Legacy

The WPA Legacy Fund

"Repurposing every stream to fund the next generation of sound."

I

Heritage Instrument Library

We provide specialized instruments—button boxes, concertinas, tenor banjos, and traditional folk brass—to school music programs in the American "Polka Belt." By filling the gap in traditional music catalogs, we ensure the unique sounds of our culture aren't lost to a lack of hardware.

Status: Identifying Partner Schools
II

Euclid Preservation Vitality

The Polka Hall of Fame is our home base. We allocate direct funding to Euclid, OH, focusing on archival-grade storage solutions (acid-free housing, climate monitoring) and operational costs to keep the museum's lights on and history accessible.

Status: Active Preservation
III

The North Bloomfield Sessions

We sponsor recording time at our North Bloomfield studio and partner facilities for emerging polka-influenced musicians. We lower the barrier to professional production, creating a new digital canon for the next century of listeners.

Status: Lab Under Construction
IV

The "Founding Pillar" Scholarship

This is our promise to the players of tomorrow. We award annual scholarships to high school musicians who are actively pursuing traditional polka or heritage-fusion music.

Whether it's for college tuition or a masterclass with a veteran accordionist, we invest in the artists who will carry the torch.
